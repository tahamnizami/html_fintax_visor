Problem / Context

Every individual and business with U.S. tax obligations must file the correct annual return with the Internal Revenue Service, and choosing the correct form — and filing it accurately and on time — is essential to staying in good standing and avoiding costly penalties. We prepare and file the full range of federal individual and business income tax returns, tailored to your residency status and entity structure.

Individual Returns

  • Form 1040 — The standard annual income tax return for U.S. tax residents, including citizens, green card holders, and individuals who meet the IRS's substantial presence test. We calculate your taxable income, apply eligible deductions and credits, and file on your behalf.
  • Form 1040-NR — Designed specifically for non-resident aliens who earn U.S.-sourced income but do not meet U.S. residency requirements. This return follows different rules and rates than the standard 1040, and we ensure it is prepared correctly to reflect your non-resident status.

Business Returns

  • Form 1065 — The information return required for partnerships and multi-member LLCs. The business itself does not pay income tax directly; instead, profits and losses pass through to the individual owners, who report their share on their personal returns. We prepare the 1065 along with the accompanying Schedule K-1 for each partner or member.
  • Form 1120 — The corporate income tax return for C-Corporations. Unlike pass-through entities, a C-Corporation is taxed as its own legal entity, separate from its shareholders, and we handle the calculation and filing of this corporate-level tax obligation.
  • Form 1120-S — The return required for S-Corporations, a hybrid structure that offers pass-through taxation similar to a partnership while retaining certain corporate liability protections. S-Corporation status comes with eligibility restrictions, and we help ensure your filing remains compliant with those rules.
