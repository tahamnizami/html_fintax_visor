Problem / Context

Before you can open a bank account, hire employees, or file a single tax return, your business and its owners typically need the proper federal identification numbers. We handle the application process for both of the following, saving you the time and confusion of navigating IRS procedures on your own:

EIN (Employer Identification Number)

Essentially a Social Security number for your business, issued directly by the IRS. An EIN is required to open a U.S. business bank account, hire employees, file federal and state tax returns, and establish business credit. We prepare and submit your EIN application and follow up with the IRS to ensure prompt issuance.

ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number)

A tax processing number issued to individuals who are required to file a U.S. tax return but are not eligible for a Social Security Number — most commonly, foreign business owners. We prepare the application, assist with the required identity documentation, and guide you through submission to the IRS.
