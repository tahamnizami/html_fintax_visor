Introduction

Choosing between an LLC and a C-Corp depends on how you want to manage ownership, tax treatment, and the long-term direction of the business.

Key differences

  • Ownership and governance
  • How profits are generally treated
  • Potential investor expectations
  • Administrative structure

Key takeaways

  • Structure choice should align with your business goals.
  • Tax implications are important but not the only factor.
  • Formation decisions can affect future operations and banking.
