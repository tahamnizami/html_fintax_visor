Problem / Context

Receiving a notice from the IRS or a state tax authority can be stressful, but most notices are resolvable with the right response prepared correctly and submitted on time. Our team reviews every notice you receive, determines the appropriate course of action, and communicates directly with the relevant tax authority on your behalf:

  • Penalty Abatement Waiver — If your business has incurred penalties for late filing or late payment, we prepare and submit a formal request for penalty relief, often citing reasonable cause or first-time abatement provisions, to reduce or eliminate the assessed penalty.
  • Additional Requirement Compliance — When the IRS or a state agency requests additional documentation, clarification, or missing filings, we compile and submit the required information promptly to bring your account back into full compliance.
  • Other Notice Resolution — For any other type of correspondence or dispute — including audits, balance-due notices, or account discrepancies — we provide comprehensive support to resolve the matter as efficiently as possible.
