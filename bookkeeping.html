Problem / Context

Accurate, up-to-date financial records are the foundation of good decision-making, successful tax filing, and investor confidence. Our bookkeeping team manages your day-to-day transaction recording, bank and credit card reconciliation, categorization of income and expenses, and monthly financial reporting — using whichever accounting platform best suits your business:

  • QuickBooks, Zoho, Sage, and Xero — We offer full setup and ongoing management across all four major platforms, including chart-of-accounts configuration, transaction entry, bank feed reconciliation, and generation of monthly or quarterly reports, so you always have a clear, current picture of your company's financial position.
  • Financial Statement Preparation (IFRS and US GAAP) — For businesses that need financial statements for investors, lenders, foreign parent companies, or regulatory purposes, we prepare formal statements — including balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements — in full accordance with either International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (US GAAP), depending on your reporting requirements.
